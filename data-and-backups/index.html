Data & Backup Information — Receipt Vault

Because Receipt Vault operates entirely on your device without a developer cloud server, please review these important facts about backups, restores, exports, and reminders.

1. Local-Only Storage & Independent Copies

All receipt data (receipt_vault.sqlite), photos (receipt_images/), and preferences (app_settings.json) reside solely on your device. Uninstalling the app, clearing app storage, losing your device, or factory resetting permanently removes in-app records unless you saved an external backup.

Recommendation: Users should keep independent copies of important receipts (original paper invoices, merchant email confirmations, or verified backups stored safely) especially for high-value purchases.

2. Backup Files (.rvbackup) Contain Sensitive Information

  • Unencrypted ZIP Format: Backup archives (receipt_vault_backup_YYYY-MM-DD.rvbackup) contain manifest.json, database/receipts.json, settings/settings.json, and your images/* receipt photos.
  • Sensitive Contents: Backup files are not encrypted or password-protected by Receipt Vault and may contain store names, purchase prices, personal notes, and photos carrying original EXIF/GPS metadata. Anyone with access to the file can read it—store backups in a trusted location.
  • Missing Source Photos: If any photo file was already missing on disk during backup export, Receipt Vault skips that missing file rather than failing the whole backup and lists it in manifest.json (missingPhotos).

3. Restoring a Backup Replaces Existing Data

Before changing your database, Receipt Vault validates the archive structure, manifest, JSON fields, image links, and CRC-32 checksums, then displays the backup summary and asks for explicit confirmation.

Overwrite Warning: Confirming restore replaces all current in-app receipts, photos, and settings with the contents of the selected backup archive. Receipts added after that backup date will be lost unless backed up first.

4. CSV Exports & Clipboard Exposure

  • Metadata Only: CSV exports omit photo files (exporting Has Photo: Yes/No instead) and cannot restore photos into Receipt Vault. Use .rvbackup for complete backups.
  • Clipboard Caution: Using Copy CSV or Copy Path places plaintext text onto your device's system clipboard, which may be readable by other apps on your device.

5. Warranty Tracking & Notification Delivery Limits

  • Informational Only: Warranty tracking and countdowns are based strictly on entered dates and do not guarantee a valid warranty claim with any merchant or manufacturer.
  • Android Settings & Lock-Screen Privacy: Local reminder delivery uses battery-friendly OS scheduling (inexactAllowWhileIdle) with NotificationVisibility.private on Android, and may be delayed or prevented by Android Doze mode, manufacturer battery savers, revoked permissions, or device clock/timezone changes.

6. Android OS Backup Disabled by App Configuration

Receipt Vault sets android:allowBackup="false" along with backup_rules.xml (android:fullBackupContent) and data_extraction_rules.xml (android:dataExtractionRules disabling cloud backup and device-to-device transfer) in its Android manifest so unencrypted app files (receipt_vault.sqlite, receipt_images/, and app_settings.json) are excluded from Android OS cloud backup and device transfers. Keep manual .rvbackup archives in a trusted location before changing devices, uninstalling the app, or clearing app storage.
