1. Plain-Language Overview

Receipt Vault is built as an offline-first, local-storage mobile application for saving purchase receipts and tracking product warranties.

  • No developer servers or accounts: Receipt Vault requires no account or sign-in, and Abid Ali / Xaviron Labs operates no remote servers that collect your receipt data.
  • No tracking SDKs or off-device transmission by the app: Release 1.0.0+1 contains no analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or Firebase services, and the Android release manifest does not declare the INTERNET permission.
  • User-controlled local storage: All receipts, notes, warranty dates, photos, preferences, exports, and backups remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to export, copy, or save files to another location or app (or email support). On Android, Receipt Vault disables Android Auto Backup and OS device transfer (Section 5); rooted environments, custom OEM tools, or other platforms remain subject to OS settings.

2. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

2.1 Receipt & Warranty Records

Stored inside the app's private SQLite database (receipt_vault.sqlite) in the application documents directory:

  • Product name
  • Store or merchant name (optional)
  • Category (optional)
  • Purchase date
  • Total amount / price
  • Warranty duration in days (optional)
  • Notes (optional)
  • Local image file reference (optional) and record creation timestamp

2.2 Receipt Photos & EXIF Metadata Notice

When you attach a receipt photo using your camera or photo gallery, a resized/compressed image copy (up to 2048 × 2048, JPEG quality 85) is saved in the app's private receipt_images/ directory.

Camera & GPS EXIF Metadata Notice: On Android, the underlying system image resizer (image_picker_android) preserves source EXIF metadata tags—which may include camera make/model, capture timestamps, and GPS location coordinates if embedded in your original photo. Receipt Vault does not read, upload, or automatically strip EXIF metadata.

2.3 App Preferences

Stored locally in app_settings.json inside the application documents directory: your selected theme mode (system, light, or dark) and warranty notification preference.

2.4 Local Timezone & Scheduled Notifications

When reminders are enabled, Receipt Vault reads your device's local IANA timezone offline (via flutter_timezone and bundled timezone data) solely to schedule local notifications at 9:00 AM local time (30, 7, 3, and 1 day prior to expiry, plus catch-up reminders where eligible).

Lock-Screen Visibility Notice: Local notifications include the product name you entered and warranty expiration date. On Android, notifications use NotificationVisibility.private to conceal sensitive receipt details on locked screens where supported by your OS lock-screen settings.

3. Permissions & OS Features Used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays opt-in local warranty reminders. Can be revoked anytime in system settings or turned off in app Settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (Android): Re-registers locally scheduled warranty alerts after your device restarts.
  • VIBRATE (Android): Vibration feedback for scheduled reminder alerts.
  • System Camera & Photo Picker: Receipt Vault declares no CAMERA or broad media/storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) in the Android release manifest. Images are obtained via explicit OS intents (ImagePickerFileProvider and, on older Android versions, Android's Google Play Services backported Photo Picker system component subject to Google/OS policies).
  • Android Storage Access Framework (SAF): Launches system document pickers (ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT and ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT) when you choose to save a CSV, save a .rvbackup file, or pick a backup for restore. Temporary staging bytes in private app cache are deleted on completion, cancellation, or next startup.

4. User-Initiated Exports, Backups & System Clipboard

  • CSV Exports (.csv): Contain unencrypted text metadata only (photos are excluded; internal local paths are omitted). Once saved outside app storage, other apps with permission to that folder can read the file.
  • System Clipboard (Copy CSV / Copy Path): Places plaintext receipt data or the saved file path onto your device's system clipboard when you tap Copy. Other apps or clipboard tools on your device may access clipboard content per your OS rules.
  • Full Backups (.rvbackup): Self-contained ZIP archives holding receipts, settings, manifest checksums, and receipt photos (including preserved EXIF tags). Backups are unencrypted and not password-protected—store them securely. See Data & Backup Information.

5. Device Backups, Security & Retention

  • Unencrypted Local Storage: Receipt Vault does not apply custom database or archive encryption; local confidentiality relies on OS app sandboxing, your device lock screen, and OS storage encryption.
  • Android OS Backup Disabled: Receipt Vault configures android:allowBackup="false", backup_rules.xml, and data_extraction_rules.xml so Android Auto Backup and OS device-transfer backup exclude local receipt records, photos, and settings.
  • Google Play Store: App installation, updates, and aggregate Play Console statistics are handled by Google LLC under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deletion & Retention: You control retention directly. Deleting receipts deletes unreferenced photos and cancels their reminders. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes all local sandbox data (but does not delete external .csv or .rvbackup files you saved elsewhere).

6. Children's Privacy

Receipt Vault is a general personal-record utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your country). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to developer servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

  • EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR) & US State Laws (e.g., CCPA/CPRA): Because all receipt data stays on your device, Xaviron Labs / Abid Ali neither sells nor shares personal information, and you may access, export (CSV / .rvbackup), modify, or delete all data directly on your device at any time.
